Using MPXAir

Maps and offline maps

Satellite and street maps, saved for flying where there's no internet.

Map types

MapBest for
Satellite (Esri World Imagery)Seeing the real terrain
Satellite + labelsSatellite with place names and roads
Street map (Esri)Roads and buildings
Topographic (Esri)Terrain and contours
OpenStreetMapDetailed community map
OpenTopoMapHiking-style terrain map

Change the map with the map-type button on the Fly or Plan map, or in Preferences → Maps.

Offline maps

  1. While online, move the Fly or Plan map to where you'll fly.
  2. Open the menu (top left) and choose Offline Maps….
  3. Choose the map type, the area (what the map shows, or 1, 3 or 10 km around it) and the zoom range. The estimate shows the number of tiles and the size.
  4. Press Download. The area is saved in the list.

Later, press Refresh on a saved area (or Refresh all) to download newer maps when you're back online.

Note

OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap don't allow bulk downloading, so they can't be saved as areas. Tiles you view in them are still cached.

Internet use

Preferences → Maps → Internet use:

  • Online: download maps as you view them, and refresh saved tiles older than N days.
  • Saver: only download tiles you don't have yet.
  • Offline: never use the internet.
