Ground station

Follow Me

Make the aircraft follow the ground station — either driven by MPXAir, or by the ground station board itself.

Follow Me from MPXAir

MPXAir sends the aircraft a new GUIDED target at your ground position, several times a second.

  1. Choose a moving ground position source: a ground station board, a USB GPS, or the computer's location. A fixed position won't work.
  2. On Ground → Follow Me, set the altitude above home, how far away to stay (0 = overhead), and how many updates per second.
  3. Press Start Follow Me and confirm. The aircraft switches to GUIDED and flies towards you.

Follow Me pauses if the aircraft leaves GUIDED, so switching mode on your transmitter always takes back control. The status line shows what it's doing.

ArduPilot Follow mode (board broadcast)

Ground station boards that can broadcast their own position (like the RFD900 + ESP32) can drive ArduPilot's built-in FOLLOW mode instead:

  1. On the aircraft, set FOLL_ENABLE = 1 and FOLL_SYSID = 255 (Parameters tab, group Follow mode (FOLL)).
  2. On Ground → Ground station type, turn on Ground station sends its position to the aircraft.
  3. Switch the aircraft to FOLLOW mode.
Safety

Test Follow Me with plenty of space and a safe altitude, and keep your transmitter in hand.
