What you need

  • A pan/tilt mount with two servos, driven by a Pololu Maestro servo controller or a serial pan/tilt board.
  • A ground position. The tracker's position is the ground position, so use the ground station's GPS or type in a fixed position.

Set it up

  1. On Ground → Antenna tracker, choose the hardware:
    • Pololu Maestro;
    • Serial pan/tilt (PWM): the board receives !!!PAN:xxxx,TLT:xxxx lines in microseconds;
    • Serial pan/tilt (degrees×10).
  2. Choose the serial port and baud rate, and the pan and tilt channels.
  3. Set the PWM minimum and maximum, the angle range each servo covers, and the trims. Reverse either servo if it moves the wrong way.
  4. Press Point north, level and check the antenna points north and level. Adjust the trims if not.
  5. Press Start tracking.

The status line shows the aircraft's bearing, elevation and distance from the tracker. Mounts with more than 120° of tilt flip over the top instead of spinning all the way round.
