Frame type

Setup → Frame type sets FRAME_CLASS and FRAME_TYPE (or the QuadPlane Q_FRAME_* settings) and the board orientation. A diagram shows the motor layout of the selected frame. Reboot after changing the frame class.

Accelerometer

  1. Press Calibrate accelerometer.
  2. MPXAir asks for six positions in turn: level, left side, right side, nose down, nose up and on its back. Hold the vehicle still and press Done — next position each time.
  3. When it says Calibration successful, reboot the autopilot.

Also available: Calibrate level only (trims roll and pitch with the vehicle level) and Simple calibration (one position, for large vehicles).

Compass

  1. Move away from metal, cars and magnets. Press Start.
  2. Slowly turn the vehicle so each side points at the ground in turn. The bars show progress for each compass.
  3. When all compasses say Success, press Accept if asked, then reboot.

Lower fitness numbers are better. Below the calibration are the compass settings (use, orientation, fitness).

Radio

  1. Switch the transmitter on. You should see the bars move.
  2. Press Calibrate and move every stick and switch to both ends.
  3. Centre the sticks, put the throttle down, and press Done. Check the values and press Save.
