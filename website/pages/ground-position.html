Where the position comes from

On the Ground tab, choose one of:

SourceUse it when
A ground station on the telemetry linkYour ground station has its own GPS and shares the USB/serial connection with the aircraft, for example an RFD900 + ESP32 board. Then choose its type.
Separate USB GPS receiverA GPS plugged in on its own port, sending NMEA. Choose the port and baud rate.
This computer's locationUbuntu's location service (Wi-Fi or a built-in GPS). Turn on Location in Ubuntu's Settings → Privacy.
A fixed position you type inA ground station that doesn't move. Use the vehicle's home fills it in for you.
Not usedMPXAir doesn't need to know where you are.

The Status row shows the position and how it is being read. The choice takes effect straight away. Press Apply after editing a port or coordinates.

Seeing it in MPXAir

  • Fly map: the blue GROUND badge with its satellite count, and a dashed line to the aircraft with the distance. Keep both in view frames them together.
  • GND GPS chip (top of the Fly map): satellites and distance to the aircraft.
  • Quick view: Distance / Bearing / Elevation from ground and Ground GPS satellites.
  • Status → Ground status: position, source, and the aircraft's distance, bearing and elevation from you.
Automatic

When MPXAir finds an RFD900 + ESP32 board on the connection, it switches the ground position to that board's GPS for you.
