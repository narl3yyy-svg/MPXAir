Getting started

Quick start tour

Try every screen in five minutes with the built-in demo drone. No hardware needed.

Connect to the demo drone

  1. Start MPXAir.
  2. At the top right, open the connection list and pick Demo drone.
  3. Press Connect. A progress window shows the link opening, the heartbeat and the parameters loading, just like Mission Planner's connection window.

Fly it

  1. Open Fly. On the Actions tab, press Takeoff, enter 20 m and confirm. The demo drone switches to GUIDED, arms and climbs.
  2. Right-click the map and choose Fly to here.
  3. Watch the HUD, the quick-view tiles and the trail. Press RTL to bring it home.

Plan a mission

  1. Open Plan and press Takeoff in the side panel.
  2. Click the map three or four times to add waypoints, then press RTL.
  3. Press Write. A window shows each item being sent and confirmed.
  4. Back on Fly → Actions, press Start Mission.

Look around

  • Setup → Motor test shows the motor layout of the demo's quad.
  • Parameters: pick a group on the left, for example Battery (BATT), and hover over a row to see what it does.
  • Logs → Download from vehicle lists the demo's logs.
  • Ground: the demo also pretends to be a ground station with its own GPS. Choose A ground station on the telemetry link to see it on the map.
Note

The demo drone is a simple pretend aircraft for learning the screens. For realistic testing, use the ArduPilot simulator.
