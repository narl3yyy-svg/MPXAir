Using MPXAir

Preferences and shortcuts

Appearance, defaults, maps, telemetry rates, and keyboard shortcuts.

General

  • Style: follow Ubuntu, light, or dark.
  • Default waypoint altitude and default takeoff altitude.
  • Flight trail on the map: 10 s … 5 min, or the whole flight.
  • Record telemetry logs (.tlog).

Maps

Map type, internet use, the refresh age for saved tiles, and a link to Offline Maps. See Maps.

Telemetry

How often the aircraft sends each kind of data (attitude, position, status, sensors, radio channels). Lower these on slow radio links. They take effect on the next connection. Here you can also update the parameter descriptions.
